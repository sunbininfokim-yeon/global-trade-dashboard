# Methodology — no-YLWS panel

## Product

**Statistics + weather-risk reference.** Not a Cavendish yield forecast.

## Why multi-year YLWS (when it arrives)

Olivares et al. 2022 used **~4 seasons of weekly** scores (2015–2018, n≈623).
A single year cannot separate site effects from disease dynamics. Request
**≥3 calendar years** of weekly YLWS/YLS if sharing becomes possible.

## What runs today (no YLWS)

1. Static FAO Banana Statistical Compendium export references (vintage labeled).
2. NASA POWER annual risk proxies per belt:
   - `sigatoka_rh_days` (RH≥90%)
   - `humid_spell_max`
   - `rain_wet` / `wet_days`
   - `wind_storm_days` (daily-mean ≥12 m/s; Central America)
3. Narrative TR4 / blowdown notes.

Risk proxies are **environment pressure**, not predicted boxes/ha.
`point` is never written.
