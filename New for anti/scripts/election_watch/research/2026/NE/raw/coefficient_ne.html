Methodology

co/efficient surveyed 1,113 likely general election voters in Nebraska on September 28-30, 2026, using mobile text responses and landline interviews. Results are weighted on age, gender, race, education, congressional district, and community type to reflect the likely 2026 general electorate. The margin of error is ±2.92 percentage points.
