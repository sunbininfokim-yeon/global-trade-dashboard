MSU Poll Shows Benson with large lead while El-Sayed/ Roger race tight

MSU Poll Shows Benson with large lead while El-Sayed/ Roger race tight
.Anti-Muslim attitudes may be playing an important role in the Senate race. 
 

East Lansing, Mich., October 26, 2026 – The Institute for Public Policy and Social Research (IPPSR) at Michigan State University released its new State of the State Survey today, showing Jocelyn Benson now leads John James 53.3% to 40.9% among likely voters in the race for governor. The Senate contest is much tighter, with Abdul El-Sayed leading Mike Rogers 48% to 45.5%. The August version of the survey had Benson up 9 percentage points on James and El-Sayed up 5.5 percentage points on Rogers. 

 

The new survey shows more splits between Benson and El-Sayed. Benson now outperforms El-Sayed, especially among independents, Wayne County residents, Catholics, Black voters, and ideological moderates. Voters under 30 are the exception: El-Sayed runs ahead of Benson among them.  

 

"The gap between the governor and Senate races has grown, with centrist voters more likely to support Benson than El-Sayed," said Dr. Matt Grossmann, Director of IPPSR. 

 

However, Islamophobia may also be a factor in the Senate race. About 38% of voters say a Muslim candidate would make them less likely to offer support. Those voters are much less likely to support El-Sayed, as are voters who rate Muslims negatively when asked their feelings toward the group.

 

Trump's unpopularity is a headwind for Republicans. Only 35% of voters rate his performance as excellent or good, and 52% say a Trump endorsement makes them less likely to support a candidate.

 

YouGov conducted interviews for the Office for Survey Research (OSR) and IPPSR between October 2–19, 2026. Each wave included 1,000 interviews; respondents were matched to a sampling frame of Michigan adults drawn from the 2023 American Community Survey, weighted by propensity score, and then post-stratified on 2024 presidential vote, gender, age, race, and education.
