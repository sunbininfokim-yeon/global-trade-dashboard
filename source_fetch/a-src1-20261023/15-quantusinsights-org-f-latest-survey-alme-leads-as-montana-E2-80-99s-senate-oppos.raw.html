Our Models and Insights

Concordia Model

The Concordia model offers a comprehensive forecast for the 2024 U.S. presidential election by integrating a variety of critical factors, each weighted according to its historical influence on electoral outcomes. This model applies advanced statistical techniques and a composite scoring method to generate predictions grounded in probability. By assessing multiple variables, Concordia provides a holistic view of the election landscape, identifying the most influential drivers of voter behavior and offering a broad forecast that captures both national and state-level dynamics.

Modus Model

The Modus model is centered on voter sentiment, focusing primarily on predicting the National Popular Vote (NPV). It draws from a combination of historical election data, real-time polling, candidate approval and favorability ratings, and key microeconomic indicators. The model is designed to simulate multiple electoral scenarios, allowing for the testing of different campaign strategies and external factors that could shift voter sentiment.


Modus Battleground is an extension of the core Modus model, zeroing in on battleground states where elections are decided. This version incorporates the historical relative bias in key swing states, adjusting for unique state-level factors like local economic conditions, voter turnout trends, and regional polling data. By doing so, it projects the most likely distribution of Electoral College votes, factoring in how small shifts in battlegrounds could determine the overall election outcome. This nuanced approach helps quantify how states like Pennsylvania, Michigan, and Wisconsin might swing in 2024, based on both national sentiment and state-specific variables.

Consensus Model

The Consensus model is an economic sentiment-based forecast that predicts voter behavior by blending a range of economic indicators. It includes critical metrics such as real disposable personal income, inflation, and the Consumer Price Index (CPI), alongside others like nonfarm payrolls and the S&P 500. These indicators are weighted to create a consolidated picture of economic sentiment, which is then mapped to voter preferences. The Consensus model is particularly focused on how economic conditions affect electoral outcomes, offering insights into how voters may react to shifts in the economy as they decide between candidates. By linking voter behavior to economic performance, it provides a crucial angle on how economic trends could sway the election.

Our Team

 Jack Murphy
CEO & Co-Founder


Jack Murphy is CEO and co-founder of Quantus Insights, where he leads business strategy, investment, and growth. Since beginning the venture with Jason Corley in August 2025, Murphy has guided the expansion of the firm’s client base, strengthened its financial resources, and invested in tools and technology to advance its polling and research capabilities.
 

An entrepreneur and business builder, Murphy also serves as CEO of Making Web LLC, which operates businesses across ecommerce, fulfillment, digital media, and survey research. Making Web ranked No. 298 on the Inc. 500 list of America’s fastest-growing private companies in 2020.
 

At Quantus, Murphy focuses on setting strategic direction, developing partnerships, and building the infrastructure and team to support the firm’s continued growth.

  

Collin Rugg
President


Collin Rugg is president of Quantus Insights, where he leads the firm’s business strategy, partnerships, and public presence. An entrepreneur, and business strategist, he has built one of the most widely followed accounts in American political media, with an audience in the millions across X and other platforms. That knowledge and reach gives Quantus a direct line to the public conversation around its polling and forecasts. Rugg advises the firm and its clients on audience, distribution, and digital strategy.

Jason Corley
Co-Founder & Director of Polling and Elections


Jason Corley is co-founder and Director of Polling and Elections at Quantus Insights, where he serves as lead pollster and is the architect of the firm’s polling and modeling operation. He launched the operation independently in 2024 and, in August of that year, joined forces with CEO Jack Murphy, who saw the potential in the work and brought the resources and backing to build it into a full-scale polling and election analytics firm. That first cycle, Quantus’ final national presidential poll finished less than one percentage point from the election margin and its October polling across Michigan, Pennsylvania, and Wisconsin averaged around one point of error, one of the two or three best records of any pollster in the 2024 cycle. Since then, Corley has continued to expand the firm’s capabilities, developing its proprietary survey pipeline, voter-file modeling, and forecasting systems in-house. 


Corley brings more than 20 years of experience across the Intelligence Community, military intelligence, cybersecurity, and data analysis. An eight-year military veteran, he deployed to Iraq as a tactical intelligence operator embedded with a U.S. Army brigade. Before co-founding Quantus, he worked in the private sector in cyber defense and consulting. He holds a bachelor’s degree in political science and a master’s degree in international political economy with a focus on trade economics, and is a CISSP-certified security professional.

Noah Griffith
Campaign Advisory & Strategy


Noah Griffith leads campaign advisory and strategy at Quantus Insights, working with candidates, committees, and advocacy clients to turn the firm’s polling and modeling into campaign plans, messaging, and voter contact strategy. A political strategist with nearly a decade of experience in campaigns, public affairs, and strategic communications, he began his career on Donald Trump’s 2016 presidential campaign and has since advised races at the local, state, and federal levels, specializing in political strategy, digital media, and communications.

Nick Weston
Political Operations & Strategy


Nick Weston manages political client operations at Quantus Insights, leading the team that delivers digital strategy, content production, and performance reporting for U.S. Senate, gubernatorial, and U.S. House campaigns.
