ALL UNH SURVEY CENTER POLLS
Democrats Foulkes & Reed Enjoy Wide Leads in Rhode Island While Matos Embattled in Lt. Governor Race 9/24/2026
Authors
UNH Survey Center
Abstract

Democrat Helena Foulkes leads Independent Ken Block and Republican Aaron Guckian in the race for governor of Rhode Island and incumbent Democratic Senator Jack Reed holds a comfortable lead over Republican Raymond McKay in the race for Senate. The race for lieutenant governor is far closer, with incumbent Democrat Sabina Matos in a statistical tie with Republican John Loughlin. Outgoing Governor Dan McKee’s job approval continues to fall. Rhode Islanders largely oppose eﬀorts to re-launch trucking tolls and bipartisan majorities doubt the new Washington Bridge will be open by the newly-announced scheduled date in November 2028.
