AJC poll: Jon Ossoff leads Mike Collins in Georgia’s U.S. Senate race
The incumbent Democrat has a comfortable lead with voters and a giant financial war chest heading into the Nov. 3 election.
Share

By Tia Mitchell
Updated Oct 21, 2026

Just a year ago, Jon Ossoff was considered the most vulnerable incumbent in the U.S. Senate — the only Democrat facing reelection in a state won by President Donald Trump in 2024.

Not anymore. 

A new poll from The Atlanta Journal-Constitution shows Ossoff with a sizable lead over his Republican challenger, U.S. Rep. Mike Collins.

Nearly 48% of voters surveyed said they would vote to reelect the Atlanta Democrat, compared with 40% who said they support Collins. Nearly 12% said they were undecided.

AJC poll: October 2026

The AJC poll of 1,000 likely general election voters in Georgia was conducted via telephone by the University of Georgia’s School of Public and International Affairs Oct. 7-15. The margin of error is 3.1 percentage points.

Likely voters had cast a ballot in the 2022 general election and/or the 2024 general election and/or the 2026 statewide primary; who indicated they were currently registered to vote in Georgia; and who were definitely or probably going to vote in the 2026 general election.
