ALL UNH SURVEY CENTER POLLS
Pappas Opens Narrow Lead Over Sununu in New Hampshire Senate Race, Race for Governor Tightens 9/24/2026
Authors
UNH Survey Center
Abstract

Democrat Chris Pappas has a narrow lead over Republican John E. Sununu in the race for U.S. Senate in New Hampshire. In the races for Congress, incumbent Democrat Maggie Goodlander holds a comfortable lead over Republican Lily Tang Williams in CD2, while Democrat Stefany Shaheen and Republican Anthony DiLorenzo are statistically tied in CD1. In the race for governor, incumbent Republican Kelly Ayotte is statistically tied with Democrat Cinde Warmington. A slim majority approve of Ayotte's handling of her job as governor. A plurality support a proposal capping local education spending. Granite Staters are concerned about the trade standoﬀ between the U.S. and Canada.
