AJC poll: Bottoms, Jackson deadlocked in race for Georgia governor

Rick Jackson, the billionaire Republican, has vastly outspent Keisha Lance Bottoms, but energized Democrats are shaping a tight race.
By Greg Bluestein
Oct 20, 2026

Republican Rick Jackson has a staggering financial edge in Georgia’s race for governor, but Democrat Keisha Lance Bottoms is campaigning in a climate shaped by President Donald Trump’s tanking approval ratings.

A new Atlanta Journal-Constitution poll shows neither candidate has managed to break away in a contest for the state’s top job, the result of which will set a new political course for Georgia.

The poll puts Jackson at 46% and Bottoms at 45%, a statistical dead heat with just over two weeks until Election Day. Another 8% of likely Georgia voters remain undecided.

The results suggest both candidates head into Nov. 3 having consolidated their partisan bases. Jackson draws support from 94% of Republicans, while Bottoms receives backing from 93% of Democrats.
