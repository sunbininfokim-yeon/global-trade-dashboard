America’s Governors

States and territories are where innovative ideas and policies are born, and successful policies become beacons of inspiration, demonstrating the power of local solutions to national challenges. America’s Governors are the architects of these policies and the custodians of our diverse and vibrant culture.

The National Governors Association provides informative gubernatorial resources, including current Governors’ biographies and terms in office, First Spouse biographies, former Governors’ biographies, Governors’ staff contact information and social media.
