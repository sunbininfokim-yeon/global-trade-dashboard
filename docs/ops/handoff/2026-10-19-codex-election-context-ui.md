Independent-spending links distinguish FEC disclosure search, the candidate-master source and the collection API; these are source lineage/search, not a fabricated individual filing URL. ## Follow-up: state office fundraising totals

User requested totals directly below the top party figures, split into governor, Senate and House. Each state office card now shows DEM and GOP campaign gross receipts and the observed overall total, immediately below the current party boxes and before the outlook. Other/independent nominees remain separate and enter the overall observed sum once. The national screen is unchanged; unrelated offices are not combined into one total.

The existing reviewed current-election roster defines the candidates. The new `usa-state-fundraising.js` reuses the candidate finance matcher and shared `linkedCampaignRow` scope guard; files and nominee appearances are deduplicated before integer-cent addition. Wrong state/office/district/cycle, future or reversed report/capture dates, ambiguous identity/report copies and unsafe integer totals are held. Primary losers, FEC-only registrations, non-election incumbents and independent expenditures never enter the sum. A current nominee's two-year gross receipts may include primary-period fundraising, loans, personal funding and transfers; this is not individual donations alone, general-election-only fundraising or transfer-netted receipts. Transfers retain the original double-count caveat.

Each party tile shows reviewed nominees and candidates with reported amounts. Partial coverage remains visibly partial; no data is null rather than $0, while an actual reported zero remains zero. Expandable candidate-original FEC links and bulk provenance retain each report end and capture day, and a failed refresh retains the original dates and previous-valid warning. A non-election office says no election; a scheduled governor office explicitly says its state campaign disclosure adapter is unconnected. No governor outside-spending amount is relabeled as campaign fundraising.
